The Method

Bring your business
into focus.

The Aperture Method moves an owner-run business from a first honest read to a living, visual intelligence platform. Each segment answers one executive question and produces one tangible deliverable the client keeps.

Who it is for: owner-run businesses, roughly $1M–$20M in revenue, place-based or data-rich. Senior-led throughout: the person who does the work is the person the client meets.

Operating principles
  • Senior-led throughout. No handoff to a junior team.
  • Fixed-fee and phase-gated. The client decides whether to continue at each gate, never an open-ended commitment.
  • Plain language. If we cannot explain why, we do not recommend on it.
  • The client owns what we build. Models, dashboards and playbooks built on their data are theirs to keep.
  • A person reviews every output. AI supports decisions; it does not make them.
  • Measured on results. We ask to be measured on whether the strategy shows up in the numbers.

How an engagement runs

1Lead & fit callQualify the business and confirm the problem is one the Method fits.
2IntakeClient completes the questionnaire. Save and resume; nothing is lost.
3Proposal & contractScope a single segment, several, or the full arc. Fixed fee per phase.
4Kickoff & data requestIssue the personalised data request and secure least-access to systems.
5Deliver segmentRun the SOP. Present a readout. Secure the phase-gate decision before continuing.
6Move to LiveStand up the Scoreboard; shift to a light recurring cadence.
7Steer & re-scoreMonthly steering, quarterly review on live data, re-scored Aperture Score to prove progress.

What powers it

Human-led, AI-enabled. Five internal specialist agents compress the mechanical work (the reading, reconciling, modelling, mapping and monitoring) so judgment, context and accountability stay with a person the client can look in the eye. The agents produce findings, never conclusions. Agent identities are internal; clients engage the Aperture modules, not the names.
