Scale

Headcount and share price by period

Headcount gives revenue and profit per employee — among the few measures that compare honestly across businesses of very different sizes. Share price applies to listed entities only.

Units: share count must be expressed in the same magnitude as the money units. With the model in $ millions, enter shares in millions — 209.1 for 209.1 million shares — so that price × shares lands in millions of dollars. Headcount is always a whole number of people.

Normalisation

Add-back schedule

A privately held income statement is prepared to minimise tax, not to present performance. Owner compensation, related-party rent, personal expenses and family on payroll all sit inside reported earnings, and none of them would survive a change of ownership. Normalising them out is what produces a figure a buyer, a lender or a board would actually use.

Discipline: every add-back is a claim that a cost would not exist under different ownership. State the amount, the reason and how well it is evidenced. An add-back marked Asserted by management is a hypothesis; one marked Documented is a fact. Diligence attacks this schedule first, and it attacks the difference between those two words.

Decomposition

Synthesis

What all of this tells us

The analysis produces a great many numbers. This page reduces them to a position: what the evidence says about the business, and what could be done to strengthen it. Draft it from the model, then rewrite it in your own words — the model can see the pattern, it cannot see the business.

Operating leverage

Break-even and margin of safety

Treating gross margin as the contribution rate and operating expenses as fixed — a simplification, and one you should state — this is the revenue the business must earn to cover its cost base, and how much room sits between that point and where it actually trades.

The assumption that cost of sales is wholly variable and operating expense wholly fixed is never exactly right. Where you know the true split, adjust the reading accordingly — the direction of travel across periods is more reliable than the level in any one of them.

Levers

What each lever is worth

Each line answers one question: if this single thing moved, what would it be worth? Sized against the most recent period, holding everything else constant. The point is not precision — it is ranking, so that effort goes where the arithmetic says it pays.

Sensitivity

Price against volume

Change in gross profit for a given price move and the volume response it provokes. Black survives the trade; maroon does not. This is the discipline that stops a price increase being argued for on margin alone.

Direction

Strengthening the position — Now, Next, Later

Sequenced, not listed. Now is the next ninety days and needs no permission; Next is this year and needs a decision; Later is contingent on what the first two reveal. Seed it from the model, then cut it down — the value of this page is in what you remove from it.
