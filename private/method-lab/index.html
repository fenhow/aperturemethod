Human in command

Fenwick How · Founder · Architect & Managing Principal

Nothing here runs on its own.

I designed the Method, I wrote the SOPs the agents execute, and I set the rubric, the weights, and the scoring criteria they score against. Every engagement is senior-led and phase-gated.

The agents do the mechanical work — assembling and reconciling data, scoring against a documented rubric, computing figures, drafting. They produce findings and artifacts. They never produce conclusions or advice. I run every interview and every readout. I name the binding constraint. I own the sequence. Nothing reaches a client until I have reviewed it, edited it, and released it — recorded with my name, a timestamp, and a diff of exactly what I approved.

Every figure in every deliverable traces to the client's own source data. No black boxes, no autonomous release, no exceptions.

Reporting to him are five specialists, each built to a defined standard of expertise, each executing a documented SOP, each answering to a human gate.
